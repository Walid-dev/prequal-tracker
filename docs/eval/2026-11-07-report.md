## Reading the results

- No model rated any answer higher than its expected label. Too lenient is the failure that matters most here, and it didn't happen.
- Opus 5 marked `cgl-complete` partial on both runs. A follow-up call showed why: it treats the stated additional-insured status as unverifiable without the endorsement document, and asks for the certificate. That's stricter than the request, which asks for these details in text, so it counts as a miss.
- Haiku 4.5 and Sonnet 5 passed every case, and 8 cases can't separate them. Sonnet 5 became the default: the stronger model for messier real answers, at about $0.006 per triage.
- No rule changed a live result, and no quote was dropped. The live models' quotes were all found in the answers. The rules stay as a guard: the mock eval in CI proves they catch an expired date marked complete and an obeyed hidden instruction.
- Spend: $0.39 for this run, plus $0.015 for the follow-up call.
